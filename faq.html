Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to the most common questions we receive from our customers. If you don't see your answer here, please Contact Us.

Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: We ship all orders via USPS Media Mail. The cost is a flat rate of $3.95 for the first book and $1.00 for each additional book. Priority shipping is available upon request!

Q: Do you buy used textbooks?
A: Yes! We buy back textbooks at the end of every semester. Please bring them to our physical location or email us a list of ISBNs for a quote.

Q: What is your return policy?
A: You may return any book within 14 days of receipt, provided it is in the same condition as sold. A 10% restocking fee applies to all returns unless the error was ours.

Q: Is it safe to use my credit card on your site?
A: Absolutely. Our server uses 128-bit encryption to protect your data. If you prefer, you can also call us at 765-555-1000 to place your order over the phone.
